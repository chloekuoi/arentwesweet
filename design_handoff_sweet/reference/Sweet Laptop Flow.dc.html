STATES
Loading and errors
To try these, set the prototype's Simulate tweak to slow or fail.
L-S4 · Wrapping
After "Wrap it up", the button reads "Wrapping…" and ignores extra clicks. Usually it takes under a second.
L-S4 · Couldn't wrap
An inline note sits above the buttons and "Wrap it up" becomes "Try again". Nothing the sender made is lost.
L-S4 · Photo extra
Click the print or drag a photo onto it, then write the caption on the white strip. The stage shows the print taped to the card.
L-R4 · Reveal with photo
The print sits beside the card in the IOU spot, and the scattered pieces move out of its way.
L-S5 · Copy blocked
If the browser blocks the clipboard, the link is highlighted and selected and a line explains the keyboard shortcut.
L-R0 · Fetching
While the link loads, the sweet rocks gently on its own with "Fetching your sweet…". Under a second on a normal connection.
L-R0 · Couldn't load
A connection failure, which is different from L-R5 "This sweet melted" (bad link). The tilted sweet stays and there's one "Try again".
LAYOUT
Layout and scale
Live renders of the prototype at three common laptop sizes, shown at 40%.
Illustrations scale, UI doesn't
The sweet, tray and note preview scale to fit the stage (max 1.2×). Text, buttons and form fields always stay full size.
Panel width
580px at 1360 and wider. 500px from 1200 to 1359. 440px from 1024 to 1199, with tighter padding and smaller titles.
Short screens
Below 860 tall: swatches 60 → 50px, note cards 68 → 56px, message box 120 → 92px, smaller gaps.
Centred stages
The arrival, unwrap and reveal screens centre on the full width. The pills keep their position relative to the sweet.
Smallest size
From 900 to 1023 wide, or below 720 tall, the whole canvas scales down evenly. Below 900 wide (tablet portrait, half-screen windows) the phone layout takes over, centred on the polka-dot paper.
Large screens
From 1440 to 1920 the panel stays at 580 and the stage widens. The sweet grows to at most 1.2×. Above 1920 × 1200 the whole canvas scales up evenly, so it never looks sparse on 2K and 4K screens.
Unwrap timing (laptop)
Unwrap runs 1.9s (phone 1.5s), so the bigger sweet has time to read. Confetti starts at 1.05s, lasts 1.35s, uses 110 pieces (phone 72) and spreads about 45% wider to fill the stage. The card rises at 2.35s.
Unwrap timing, why
At laptop size the bows travel about twice as far on screen. At phone timing that feels like a snap rather than a reveal. To compare, switch the prototype's Motion tweak to phone.
